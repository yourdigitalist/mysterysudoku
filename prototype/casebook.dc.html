How to play
Place every suspect on the floor plan. Use the clues to work out who was alone with the victim.
Rules
One person per row. One person per column.
Hatched cells are blocked. Nobody stands there.
Every clue is true and there is only one answer. No guessing needed.
The victim was alone in a room with one other person. That person is the murderer.
Controls
Tap a suspect to select them.
Place: tap a cell to put the selected suspect there. Tap again to pick them up.
Note: tap a cell to jot their initial. Press and hold still places.
Mark X rules a cell out for everyone.
Erase clears a cell. Hold it to clear the board.
Hint nudges you, explains, then places.
Keywords
Beside: directly left, right, above or below, in the same room.
On / in: standing on that object.
North / south of: any row above or below it.
East / west of: any column to the right or left of it.
Diagonal to: on either diagonal line through them, across the whole board.
Alone with: only those two were in the room.
Nobody: includes the victim.
